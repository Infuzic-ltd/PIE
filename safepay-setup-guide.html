Setting up Safepay for PIE Real Estate

This turns on card payments for the "Feature My Listing" fee on the website. It takes about 15 minutes to start testing, and 1–2 weeks to go live once your documents are ready. Nothing on the site breaks in the meantime — featured requests are simply saved and followed up on manually until this is switched on.

Have these ready

Saves a back-and-forth with Safepay's review team
  • ✓CNIC (front & back), colour scanFor the owner, or anyone holding 10%+ of the business.
  • ✓Bank account maintenance certificateFrom the bank PIE gets paid into — confirms the account is real and active. Ask your bank; it usually takes 1–2 business days to issue.
  • ✓NTN & business registration documentsOnly if PIE is registered as a company rather than trading as an individual.
None of this is needed for step 1 below — that part is free to test today with no paperwork at all.

The steps, in order

1
Test mode · no paperwork

Create a Sandbox account

Go to sandbox.api.getsafepay.com and sign up with an email, a password, and PIE's store name. This is a practice account — no bank details, no real money.

2
Test mode

Copy the Sandbox keys into PIE's CRM

Inside the Sandbox dashboard, open Developers → API. Copy the API Key and Secret Key shown there and paste them into the CRM (see the panel below). Leave the Environment set to Sandbox for now.

Submit a test "Feature My Listing" request on the website using Safepay's dummy card numbers to confirm the whole flow works before any real money is involved.

3
Going live

Create a Production account

When you're ready to accept real payments, sign up separately at getsafepay.com. This is a distinct account from Sandbox — different login, different keys.

4
Going live

Complete the Merchant Onboarding Form

After logging in to the Production account, Safepay prompts you to complete onboarding. Upload the documents from the checklist above. Safepay typically replies within 48 hours, though gathering paperwork can take 1–2 weeks if anything needs chasing.

5
Going live

Switch PIE's CRM over to Production

Once Safepay confirms approval, go to Developers → API in the Production dashboard, copy those keys, and replace the Sandbox ones in the CRM. Change Environment to Production and save — PIE is now charging real customers.

Where the keys go

In the CRM

Settings page

Log in to the CRM as an admin and open the Settings page from the sidebar. There's a "Safepay Payment Gateway" card with three fields:

Environment
Sandbox / Production
API / Client Key
pub_… or similar
Secret Key
sec_…
Featured Listing Fee
PKR amount, editable anytime
Direct link once logged in: /crm/settings/
⚠

One thing to double-check in your dashboard: some Safepay accounts show a separate "Webhook Secret Key" next to the general Secret Key under Developers.

If you see two different secrets, send both to whoever maintains the CRM — one extra field needs to be wired in so payment confirmations verify correctly. If there's only one secret shown, the current setup already covers it.
